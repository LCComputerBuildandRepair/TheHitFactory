Lessons

Private and small group instruction for baseball and softball players, available all year by appointment.

Built around each athlete

Every session is tailored to the player's current skill level and goals, from learning the fundamentals to sharpening advanced technique.

  • Hitting mechanics and approach
  • Pitching and throwing fundamentals
  • Defensive technique and footwork
  • Mental preparation and confidence

Two ways to train

Private lessons

One athlete and one instructor. Individual coaching and drills built for one player.

Small group lessons

2 to 4 athletes. A team-like setting that still keeps the focus on each player's skills.
