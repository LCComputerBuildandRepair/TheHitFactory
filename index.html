Everything you need to get better, under one roof

The building on Plymouth Street was renovated from the ground up so every player gets good turf, good machines and room to work.

Batting cages

Netted tunnels with pitching machines set for baseball or softball. Come in for a quick round of swings or book a full hour to grind.

Pitching mounds

Throw a real bullpen off a real mound, in January, without a coat.

Private lessons

One-on-one work on hitting, pitching, fielding and the mental side of the game with coaches who have been there.

Team rentals

Bring the whole roster. Rent the facility for winter workouts, pre-season practice or a rainout makeup.

Birthday parties & groups

Looking for something the kids will actually remember? Ask us about booking the cages for a party.

Booking takes about a minute

  1. Pick a time. Open our booking page and choose a cage, mound or lesson slot that works for you.
  2. Pay online. Lock in your spot so it is waiting when you pull in.
  3. Show up and swing. Bring your bat and glove. We have the balls and the machines.
