About The Hit Factory

A family-founded program built on a love for baseball, softball and our community.

Meet the founders

Nathan and Codi Fisher have lived in Lexington their whole lives. As a family they're deeply involved in youth sports, and The Hit Factory has been their dream for a long time.

Nathan has coached youth baseball at many levels, including the North Central Titans travel program and the Lexington High School Baseball staff. He coaches with an emphasis on fundamentals, sportsmanship, and confidence built through discipline and hard work.

Codi is a big supporter of youth development on and off the field, whether she's cheering from the stands or backing local dancers.

Our mission

Help young athletes develop their skills, build confidence and reach their full potential on and off the field.

A top-tier facility

We renovated our building so every athlete trains in a great environment with cages, mounds and professional-grade equipment.

Expert coaching

Experienced instructors who focus on mechanics, strength, agility and mental preparation.

Programs for every athlete

Private and group lessons, year-round rentals for teams and individuals, plus skill-focused clinics and camps.

A real community

Our goal isn't only strong players. It's a community built on passion, respect and opportunity.
